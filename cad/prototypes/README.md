# Early 3D-printed prototypes

I designed these four parts for the first 3D-printed RakFlex prototype: a nut and screw to attach it to the gym rack, and two mating halves to hold the phone.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [prototype-rack-mount-screw.step](prototype-rack-mount-screw.step) | Screw portion of the rack attachment |
| [prototype-rack-mount-nut.step](prototype-rack-mount-nut.step) | Mating nut that secures the rack attachment |
| [prototype-phone-holder-male-jaw.step](prototype-phone-holder-male-jaw.step) | Male half of the prototype phone holder |
| [prototype-phone-holder-female-jaw.step](prototype-phone-holder-female-jaw.step) | Female half of the prototype phone holder |

Open the `.step` files in a STEP-compatible CAD viewer or editor.

A separately sourced gooseneck connected the rack attachment to the phone holder. No gooseneck model is included here.

For the later product, I moved the black rack-mount component to CNC-machined aluminum and replaced my printed phone holder with a smaller, cheaper purchased holder. These files show the early printed designs; they are not the final product's manufacturing drawings.
